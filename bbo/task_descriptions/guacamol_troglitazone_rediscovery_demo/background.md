# Background

`guacamol_troglitazone_rediscovery_demo` is a GuacaMol-derived rediscovery task.
It decodes fixed-length SELFIES token configurations to SMILES and scores them by ECFP4 Tanimoto similarity to Troglitazone.

The task is deterministic and local; the ZINC archive is used only to seed the SELFIES vocabulary.
